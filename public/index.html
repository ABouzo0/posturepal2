How it works

  1. It watches

    MediaPipe tracks your shoulders and head right in the browser. Nothing is uploaded or recorded.

  2. It nudges

    Slouch past the time you set and you get an iMessage, plus an optional spoken reminder from ElevenLabs.

  3. It reports back

    When you stop, you get a short recap: how long you sat upright and how that compares to your usual.
